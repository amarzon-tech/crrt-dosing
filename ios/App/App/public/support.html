CRRT Rx Support

CRRT Rx is an educational CRRT prescribing tool for nephrologists, published by Wellness Tech LLC. It calculates effluent dose, heparin, and Prismocitrate 18 citrate rates on your device.

Contact

Email amardamodar@gmail.com for support or questions about the app.

How the app works

It works fully offline, with no login, no ads, and no data collection. Sources for each formula are listed in the in-app About sheets.
