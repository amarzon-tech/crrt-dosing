CRRT Rx Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

We collect no data

CRRT Rx does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Inputs you type (such as weight or blood flow) are used only on the screen to calculate a prescription and are never saved or sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

Nothing is written to local storage, iCloud, or a server. The dark-mode toggle lasts only while the app is open. Deleting the app removes it completely.

No network use

The iOS app works fully offline and does not send data over the internet. Optional citation links (for example to ClinicalTrials.gov) open only if you tap them, in the device browser.

Medical disclaimer

For nephrologists. Educational use only; not a substitute for clinical judgment or your unit protocol.
